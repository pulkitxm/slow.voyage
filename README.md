# slow.voyage

An endless scenic drive in your browser. No destination, no timer, no score: pick a
car, pick a landscape, and take the long way home.

Play it at [slow.voyage](https://slow.voyage). It is free, needs no account, and keeps
working offline once it has loaded.

Highly inspired by [slowroads.io](https://slowroads.io/) (big fan of the game!!).

## What is in it

Seven landscapes, from alpine passes and red canyons to northern lakes and monsoon
jungle. Seven vehicles, from a grand tourer and a supercar to a pickup, a motorcycle
and a coach. Three seasons, six skies including a live day cycle, weather from clear to fog and
storms, autodrive, a photo mode and a minimap of the road ahead.

## How we built it

**A world that is never stored.** The road, hills, lakes, forests and waterfalls are
grown from a seed as you drive. The road never repeats and the country around it is
open in every direction, built around the car and released behind it.

**Everything is made, not downloaded.** Every car, walker, animal, tree, plant, rock
and road surface is generated by scripts in this repository, modelled and baked in
Blender or painted from noise. Nothing is borrowed from an asset store.

**Sound is synthesized live.** Each engine is modelled cylinder by cylinder with its
own firing order, so the V8 burbles, the V12 howls and the diesel clatters. Tyres,
wind, gravel and the birds, insects and water around you are all generated as you
drive, with no recordings.

**Light that adapts to the machine.** It runs on Three.js in any modern browser. Ultra
quality adds HDR lighting, ambient occlusion, bloom, tyre smoke, dust, rain spray,
sparks and headlight beams, and quietly steps itself down before it ever stutters.

**The trailer is the game.** The launch video and stills were rendered by the game
itself, frame by frame, with its own engine sound as the soundtrack.
